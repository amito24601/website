I knew it all along: Why The Past Seems Predictable

Hindsight bias

BiasesMemory

Have you ever looked back at something that happened and thought, “I knew it all along”?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore Hindsight Bias — the tendency to see an outcome as more predictable after we already know what happened. We look at how our memories and interpretations can shift, making the past seem more obvious than it really was.

But if the outcome feels inevitable in hindsight, how accurately do we remember what we thought beforehand? Discover how hindsight bias can shape our confidence, distort our memories, and influence the way we judge our own decisions — and those of others.
