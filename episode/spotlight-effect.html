All eyes on me? Why We Think Everyone Is Watching

Spotlight effect

BiasesSocial dynamics

Have you ever worried that everyone noticed something embarrassing you did — when they may not have noticed at all?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Spotlight Effect — the tendency to overestimate how much other people notice, remember, or pay attention to us. We look at why our own experiences can feel so visible, and how that can shape the way we act around others.

But what if the spotlight we feel is mostly in our own minds? Discover how the Spotlight Effect can influence our confidence, heighten self-consciousness, and remind us that other people may be far less focused on us than we imagine.
