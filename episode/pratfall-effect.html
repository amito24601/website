Perfectly imperfect: Why Mistakes Can Make Us More Likeable

Pratfall effect

JudgementSocial dynamics

Can making a mistake actually make someone more likeable?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Pratfall Effect — the psychological phenomenon suggesting that a competent person can become more appealing after making a small, relatable mistake. We look at how imperfections can influence the way others perceive us, and why being flawless isn’t always the advantage we imagine.

But why can a little clumsiness make someone seem more human? Discover how competence, vulnerability, and small mistakes can shape our impressions — and why sometimes, being imperfect can bring us closer to others.
