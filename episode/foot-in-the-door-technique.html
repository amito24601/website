Just one small favour: How Tiny Yeses Lead to Bigger Ones

Foot-in-the-door technique

Decision makingSocial dynamics

Why can agreeing to one small request make it easier to say yes to a much bigger one later?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Foot-in-the-Door Technique — a persuasion strategy built around the idea that small commitments can make people more likely to agree to larger requests. We look at how the choices we make can influence what we’re willing to do next.

But how does a simple “yes” become the beginning of something bigger? Discover how commitment, consistency, and the way requests are presented can shape our decisions — often before we realise how far we’ve been drawn in.
