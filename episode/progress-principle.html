Small wins, big changes: The Psychology of Progress

Progress principle

Motivation

Why can making a little progress feel so much more motivating than waiting for one big breakthrough?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Progress Principle — the idea that making meaningful progress, even in small steps, can have a powerful effect on our motivation and sense of wellbeing. We look at how everyday forward movement can shape the way we feel about our work and what keeps us going.

But what if the secret to staying motivated isn’t a huge achievement, but simply seeing that we’re moving forward? Discover how small wins can influence our mindset, strengthen our motivation, and help us build momentum over time.
