Same facts, different choice: How Words Change Decisions

Framing effect

BiasesDecision making

Can changing the way something is described change how we feel about it — even when the facts stay exactly the same?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Framing Effect — the psychological phenomenon in which our decisions are influenced by how information is presented. We look at how emphasizing potential gains or losses can shift our perceptions, preferences, and choices.

But are we responding to the facts themselves, or to the way they’re framed? Discover how language and presentation can shape our judgments, influence our decisions, and reveal why the same information can lead us to very different conclusions.Studies and links:
