The Phone in the Room Study: Is Your Phone Distracting You Without You Knowing?

Phone-in-the-room study

Attention

Can simply having your phone nearby make it harder to give something your full attention?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Phone-in-the-Room Study — research examining how the mere presence of a smartphone may affect our attention and thinking, even when we aren’t actively using it. We look at what this reveals about the subtle ways technology can compete for our mental resources.

But if a phone can influence us without making a sound or lighting up, how much attention are we really giving to the moment in front of us? Discover how the presence of technology may shape our focus — and what it means for the way we connect, concentrate, and think.
