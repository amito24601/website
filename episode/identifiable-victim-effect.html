The Identifiable Victim Effect: Why One Story Moves Us More

Identifiable victim effect

BiasesDecision making

Why can one person’s story move us more than the suffering of thousands?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Identifiable Victim Effect — the tendency for us to feel more compassion and willingness to help when we can connect with a specific person, rather than a large group represented by statistics. We look at how personal stories and vivid details can shape our emotional responses to those in need.

But why can a single face feel more urgent than thousands of lives described in numbers? Discover how the way suffering is presented influences our empathy, our decisions, and the people we choose to help.
