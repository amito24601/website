Becoming your expectations: How Beliefs Shape Reality

Self-fulfilling prophecy

JudgementSocial dynamics

Can believing something will happen make us behave in ways that bring it about?

In this episode of Circuit Breaker: Rewiring Your Decisions, we explore the Self-Fulfilling Prophecy — the process by which our expectations can influence our behaviour, helping turn a belief into reality. We look at how what we anticipate can shape the choices we make, the way we interact with others, and the outcomes we experience.

But how often do our predictions describe the future — and how often do they help create it? Discover how expectations can shape our actions, reinforce our beliefs, and influence the direction our lives take.
